## Comments

When you write a comment, we store the name you enter, your text and the time. We read every comment before it appears; after that, it is publicly visible with your name below the post. We do not store your IP address or your email address. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in enabling discussion below our posts (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Your comment stays as long as the post is online; we delete it at any time on your request.

An email with your name and an excerpt of your text notifies us of each new comment. It is sent through the Resend service of Plus Five Five, Inc., San Francisco, USA, which acts on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR). Resend is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).

As soon as you use the form, we set a session cookie (kirby_session). It protects the form against forged requests and expires after two hours. The cookie is strictly necessary for sending the comment you requested (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG).

To protect against spam, we use Cloudflare Turnstile from Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. The check loads only when you first use the comment form. Cloudflare then processes your IP address and details about your browser, device and interaction to tell humans from automated programs; when you submit, our server sends your IP address to Cloudflare for verification. Cloudflare does not receive the name or text of your comment. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in protecting the form against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); what Turnstile stores on your device is strictly necessary for sending the comment you requested (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG). Cloudflare may transfer the data to the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR) and has additionally agreed to the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). Learn more in the [Turnstile privacy notice](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/).
